FAQ

What is Larken?
A mission alarm: you complete a real task to silence it, which kills the snooze habit and actually gets you out of bed.
Can I really not snooze it?
There's no snooze button, and the alarm keeps coming back until your mission is done. Your phone's system Stop button always exists — we don't hide that — but the app re-fires until you've actually completed the mission.
Is my camera and data private?
Yes. Photo missions are verified entirely on your device (Apple Vision / on-device ML Kit) — nothing is ever uploaded, no cloud, no servers. Photos are deleted right after they're checked, unless you keep the optional wake-up photo diary — and even then they never leave your phone.
Does it need internet or an account?
Neither. Larken works fully offline and never asks you to sign up.
What does it cost?
There's a 3-day free trial, then a subscription. Current pricing is shown in the App Store or Google Play.
What devices are supported?
iPhone (iOS 26 and up). Android is coming soon.
